# Post 4 — Your website is your shop door

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Your website is your shop door. If it sticks, people don't wait. They walk away.

I see this a lot. A business owner spends on ads and posts every day, then sends all that attention to a site that loads slowly, buries the next step, or makes people hunt for a phone number.

Nobody argues with a stuck door. They just leave quietly, and you never find out they came.

Marketing gets people to the shop. The website decides whether they walk in.

This week, open your own site on your phone, on mobile data, and try to do the one thing you want a customer to do. If you get stuck, so do they.

What is the first thing a visitor should do on your website, and can they do it in under 10 seconds?
