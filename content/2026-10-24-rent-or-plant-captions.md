# Post: Ads rent attention. SEO plants it.

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Ads are rent. SEO is a tree. You need to know which one you are paying for.

I see a lot of business owners put their whole marketing budget into ads. It works while the money flows. The day the budget stops, the leads stop too. The lamp goes dark.

SEO is slower. You plant it, you wait, and for a while it looks like nothing is happening. But a page that ranks keeps bringing people in, month after month, without a fresh payment each time.

Ads and SEO are not enemies. Ads give you speed today. SEO gives you something you still own next year.

This week, look at where your enquiries came from last month. Count how many would still arrive if you paused every ad.

If you paused your ads tomorrow, what would happen to your enquiries?
