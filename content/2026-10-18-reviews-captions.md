# Post 10 — Reviews are word of mouth at scale

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

One happy customer tells one friend. A hundred reviews tell everyone who searches for you.

I see this a lot. Business owners treat reviews as a nice extra, something to collect "when there is time". But for a new customer deciding whether to trust you, reviews are often the first thing they read.

Word of mouth has always worked. A review just lets it work while you sleep. A whisper reaches one person. Five stars on your profile reach the whole queue.

This week, pick your three happiest customers and ask each of them for a review. Do it right after a good moment, and make it easy by sending the direct link.

When did you last ask a customer for a review?
