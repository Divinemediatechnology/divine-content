Your website homepage should answer one question in five seconds: "Is this for me?"

Most business owners write the homepage about themselves. Our story, our values, our team. The visitor came with a problem and has to dig for it.

I see this a lot. People land, scroll once, feel unsure and leave. They never tell you why.

Say who you help and what you fix, in plain words, at the very top. Then show the next step: one button, one action.

This week, show your homepage to someone who does not know your business. Give them five seconds. Ask what you sell and who it is for.

If they cannot tell you, that is your first edit.

What does the top line of your homepage say today?
