Your business probably has three different phone numbers online, and your customers notice.

An old number on a directory. A new one on Instagram. A third on the website that nobody updated.

When details do not match, people hesitate. They wonder if you are still open, still active, still the right place to call.

It is a small fix that costs nothing. Your name, address, phone number and opening hours should read exactly the same everywhere a customer might find you.

This week, search your business name and write down every place it shows up. Fix the ones that are wrong.

When did you last check what Google shows about you?
