Topic: Pricing: discounting trains customers to wait
Type: honest "what does not work" (general, no invented client story)

Every discount teaches your customers to wait for the next one.

I see this a lot. Sales are slow, so a business runs an offer. Orders come in, which feels like proof it works. A month later the same customers are waiting for the next offer before they buy.

A discount does not only lower one price. It tells people what your real price is, and it is the lower one.

That does not mean never discount. It means being clear about why. A launch offer with an end date, a bundle that adds value, or a thank-you for loyal customers all send a different message than a sale every time things go quiet.

This week, look at your last three offers. Did they bring new customers, or just move existing ones forward? If you cannot tell, that is worth fixing before the next one.

When did a discount last work well for your business, and why?
