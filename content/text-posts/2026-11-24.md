Judging a campaign after three days is how good marketing gets switched off.

I see this a lot. A business owner starts ads or a new posting plan, checks the numbers on day three, sees little, and stops.

Three days is not enough time for anyone to notice you, trust you and act.

Before you start anything, decide two things: how long you will run it, and what result would make you change course. Write both down.

Then leave it alone until that date. Judge it on enquiries, not on how it feels in week one.

Quitting early costs you the money you already spent and the answer you were paying for.

What is the last thing you stopped before it had a fair chance?
