Most business owners judge their marketing by how busy it looks, not by what it costs to win one customer.

A feed that is full of posts feels like progress. But a post is not a customer.

Here is the question I wish more owners asked: roughly how much do I spend, in money and in time, to get one new customer?

If you cannot answer, you cannot tell which channel is working and which one is just keeping you busy.

This week, write down what you spent on marketing last month and how many new customers came from it. Even a rough guess is better than none.

Do you know your number today?
