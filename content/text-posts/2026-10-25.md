# Text post, 2026-10-25 19:00 IST: Collecting reviews: ask at the happy moment

The best time to ask for a review is the moment your customer is happiest, not a week later.

Many business owners wait. They plan to send a follow-up email "when things calm down". By then the good feeling has faded, and the request feels like a chore.

Think about when a customer says "this is great" out loud. That is the moment. It might be when you deliver the work, when a problem gets solved, or when they thank you on a call.

Right then, ask. Keep it small: one sentence, one link, one minute of their time.

Three things that help:

1. Ask in person or on the call first, then send the link straight away.
2. Make the link open directly on the review page, not your homepage.
3. Say thank you when they post it.

You do not need dozens of reviews to start. A steady few, collected at the right time, do more than a big push once a year.
