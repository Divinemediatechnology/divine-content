Marketing does not need a big plan. It needs a boring weekly routine.

Most business owners I talk to do marketing in bursts. A busy month, then silence. Then a panic post.

Customers do not notice your best week. They notice whether you are still around next month.

Pick one small task and tie it to a fixed day. Every Monday, share one useful answer to a question customers ask you. Every Friday, reply to every review and comment.

Small and regular beats big and rare.

This week, choose one marketing task you can keep up for the next three months. What is it?
