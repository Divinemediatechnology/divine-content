Your customers already wrote your best marketing line. You just have not read it.

Most business owners describe their service in their own words. "Integrated solutions." "End-to-end support." Nobody talks like that.

Customers talk differently. They say "I was tired of chasing people" or "I just wanted someone to answer the phone."

I see this a lot. The words that win the sale are sitting in reviews, emails and WhatsApp messages.

This week, read your last ten messages from happy customers. Pick the phrase that shows up most and put it on your homepage, word for word.

Plain beats clever when someone is deciding in five seconds.

What is the one phrase your customers keep using about you?
