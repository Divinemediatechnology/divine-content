A busy week is not the same as a productive marketing week.

Many business owners tell me they posted, replied, designed and boosted all week. Then I ask what the week was meant to achieve, and there is a pause.

Activity feels like progress. It is not. Progress is one clear result moving in the right direction.

Pick one number for the month: enquiries, bookings or calls. Then look at every marketing task and ask, "does this move that number?" If it does not, it can wait.

This week, write your one number on a sticky note and put it where you work.

What is the one number your marketing should move this month?
