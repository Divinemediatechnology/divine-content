# Text post, 2026-10-28 19:00 IST: Saying "I don't know" to a client

"I don't know yet" is a better answer than a confident guess.

I see this a lot in agency life. A client asks whether a campaign will work, or how fast, and there is pressure to sound sure. So people promise a number, and later have to explain why it was wrong.

Marketing has too many moving parts for certainty. What you can offer is honesty and a plan.

"I don't know yet, but here is how we will find out" does three things. It protects the client's budget. It shows you are not guessing. And it makes the next update more believable, because they know you tell the truth when the news is slow.

The same works from the client side. If an agency never says "I am not sure", ask yourself what they are not telling you.

This week, next time you feel pushed to promise a result, say what you know, what you do not, and when you will know more.

When did an honest "I don't know" last build trust for you?
