A slow reply loses more leads than a weak ad ever will.

When someone sends an enquiry, they are usually looking at two or three businesses at the same time. The first one that answers properly often gets the conversation.

I see this a lot. Business owners spend weeks on their ads and then reply to the enquiry two days later.

You do not need a long, perfect answer. A quick "Thanks, got your message, here is what happens next" already puts you ahead.

This week, check how long it takes your business to reply to a new enquiry. Then set one rule, for example every enquiry gets a first reply within two hours in working time.

How fast does your business reply to a new enquiry today?
