Topic: Reels vs static: test, don't guess
Type: myth vs reality (general, no invented client story)

"Reels always beat static posts" is a rule of thumb, not a fact about your business.

I see this a lot. A business owner hears that video gets the reach, so everything becomes a reel. Or the opposite: "our audience likes clean graphics", so nothing else is ever tried. Both are guesses that hardened into policy.

Reach and results are different things. A reel can be seen by many people who will never buy. A plain static post can quietly bring in the right enquiry.

Do a simple test. For the next four weeks, post both formats on the same topic. Keep everything else the same. Then look at what you actually care about: enquiries, saves, replies, not only views.

You may find video wins. You may not. Either way you will know instead of guess.

What did you assume about your content that you have never actually tested?
