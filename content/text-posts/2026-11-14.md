Most business websites ask visitors to do five things at once.

Call us. Follow us. Read the blog. Sign up. Check our offers.

When everything is a next step, nothing is. People leave without doing any of it.

Pick the one action that matters most for your business. A call, a form, a WhatsApp message. Make that button obvious on every page and cut the rest down to quiet links.

I see this a lot when business owners ask why traffic does not turn into enquiries. It is rarely a traffic problem. It is a "what do I do now?" problem.

This week, open your homepage on your phone and ask a friend to find how to contact you. Time them.

What is the one action you most want a visitor to take on your site?
