# Text post, 2026-10-29 19:00 IST: A monthly report should end in a decision

A marketing report that ends with "see you next month" has failed.

I see this a lot. A report arrives with charts, percentages and arrows pointing up. Everyone nods. Nothing changes, because nobody was asked to decide anything.

A report is only worth the time if it ends with a choice. Do more of this. Stop that. Test one new thing.

Charts can look impressive and still say nothing about what to do next.

A good report has a short list: what worked, what did not, and the one change we are making this month. That is it. The rest is detail for anyone who wants it.

If you are a business owner, you are allowed to ask for this. "What should we do differently because of this?" is a fair question to put to any agency or marketer.

This week, open your last report and write the one decision it should have led to. If you cannot find one, ask for a different report.

What is the last decision a report actually changed for you?
