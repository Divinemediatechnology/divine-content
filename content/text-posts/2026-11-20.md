Most business owners know their price. Few can say what the customer gets for it.

When a prospect says "that is expensive", they are rarely comparing numbers. They are comparing a number against a picture in their head, and you have not given them a picture.

So the price sits alone, and alone it looks big.

I see this a lot in proposals. A line item, a total, and nothing about what changes for the customer after they say yes.

Before your next quote, write one sentence at the top: "After this, you will be able to ___." Then show the price.

If you cannot finish that sentence, the customer cannot either.

What is the sentence at the top of your last quote?
