Topic: Slow replies lose leads
Type: mistake business owners make (general, no invented client story)

When someone sends you an enquiry, the first reply is rarely the one that wins the job. The fast reply is.

I see this a lot. A business owner spends money to get a message in the inbox, then answers it the next morning. By then the person has already messaged two other businesses.

Nobody is being rude. They are simply buying from whoever answers first, while they still remember why they asked.

You do not need a big team to fix this. Pick one hour in the morning and one in the evening that are only for replying. Write a short first reply that says you got the message and when they will hear the full answer.

Speed does not replace a good answer. It only gets you to the point where a good answer can matter.

How long does a new enquiry wait for a reply at your business right now?
