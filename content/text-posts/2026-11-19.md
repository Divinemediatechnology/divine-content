Your reply to a bad review is not for the person who wrote it.

When a business gets a harsh review, the instinct is to defend. Or to go quiet and hope nobody sees it.

But the reviewer has already made up their mind. The people reading next month have not.

They are checking one thing: what happens when something goes wrong here?

A calm, short reply answers that. Thank them, own what is true, offer to fix it offline. No arguments, no essays.

This week, open your most recent unanswered review and write one honest reply. Keep it under four lines.

When did you last read your own reviews from a stranger's point of view?
