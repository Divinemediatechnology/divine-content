Hiding your prices costs you more enquiries than showing them.

Many business owners tell me they keep prices off their website so people will "call and ask". Most visitors will not call. They will just open the next tab.

A price range does one useful thing. It filters. The people who contact you already know roughly what you cost, so the conversations are shorter and better.

You do not need a full price list. A line like "projects usually start from" and "most customers spend between" is enough, as long as it is true.

This week, add one honest price range to your homepage or your Instagram bio and see how the enquiries change.

Do you show your prices, and has it helped or hurt?
