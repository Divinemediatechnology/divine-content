A busy week is not the same as a working marketing plan.

Many business owners can list everything they posted, boosted and designed this month. Few can say which of it was meant to bring a customer.

I see this a lot: activity feels like progress, so nobody asks what the activity was for.

Before you approve anything new, finish this sentence: "This exists to get more ___." If you cannot fill the blank, pause it.

This week, go through your last ten pieces of content and write the blank next to each one. You may be surprised how many stay empty.

How many of your last ten posts had a clear job?
