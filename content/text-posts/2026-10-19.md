Topic: Rebrand when sales are slow = wrong fix
Type: mistake business owners make (general, no invented client story)

When sales are slow, the first idea is often a new logo.

I see this a lot. A new look feels like progress. It is visible, it is exciting, and it is something you can finish this month. Slow sales feel vague and uncomfortable, so a rebrand becomes the easier job.

But a logo rarely explains why people are not buying. The reasons are usually less glamorous. The offer is unclear. Nobody follows up on enquiries. The website does not say what to do next. A new coat of paint on any of those changes very little.

Before you spend on a rebrand, ask three plain questions. Do people understand what we sell within five seconds? Do enquiries get a quick reply? Is it obvious what to do next on our site?

Fix what those answers show. Rebrand later, when the business has outgrown how it looks.
