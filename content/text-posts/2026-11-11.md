Likes do not pay your rent. Enquiries do.

A lot of business owners open their analytics, look at the likes, and feel good or bad for the day. Then they close it without a single decision.

I see this a lot: a post can get plenty of likes and bring no customers, and a quiet post can bring the one call that matters. Likes tell you people noticed. Enquiries tell you people want to buy.

So change what you count. Every week, write down how many people messaged, called or filled in a form, and where they came from.

This week, open a note and start that list. After a month, you will know which posts are actually worth repeating.

What is the one number you check first on Monday morning?
