Your Instagram bio is a sales page, and most are wasted.

When someone likes a post and taps your name, you get about three seconds. They read the bio and decide if you are for them.

I see a lot of bios that say "Dreamer. Coffee lover. Making things happen." Nice, but a stranger cannot tell what you sell or who you help.

A good bio answers three things: what you do, who it is for, and what to do next. One line each.

This week, open your own profile as if you were a new customer. If you cannot tell in three seconds what you offer, rewrite it and add one clear action, like "Message us to book".

What does your bio say about your business right now?
