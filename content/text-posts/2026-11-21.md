Ask three people in your team what counts as a "lead" and you will get three answers.

One counts every form fill. One counts only calls. One counts anyone who liked a post.

Then the monthly report says "forty leads", and nobody knows if that is good news.

I see this a lot. The number is not wrong. The word is just undefined.

Before your next report, write one line: "A lead is someone who ___." Make it a person who could realistically buy. Count only those.

The number will probably drop. It will also finally mean something.

What is your definition of a lead today?
