# Text post, 2026-10-26 19:00 IST: Why "go viral" is not a plan

"Make it go viral" is a wish, not a plan.

I hear it a lot from business owners. Fair enough, everyone has seen a reel with a million views. But nobody can schedule that. Not me, not any agency.

Even when a post does blow up, a big share of the viewers will never be your customers. They are not in your city, not in your price range, or not looking for what you sell. Views are loud. Sales are quiet.

A plan looks more boring. Know exactly who you want to reach. Say one clear thing to them. Show up on the same platform every week. Make it easy to contact you.

If one post happens to travel, good. Your page is ready to turn the attention into enquiries. If it does not, you still have a steady flow of the right people.

This week, write down the one type of customer you want more of. Then check whether your last five posts were made for them.

Who was your last post actually made for?
