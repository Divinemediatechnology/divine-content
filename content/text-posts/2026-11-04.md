Ask every new customer one question: "How did you find us?"

Most business owners guess where their customers come from. They credit the last post they liked, or the platform they enjoy using.

I see this a lot. Money goes into three channels, and nobody knows which one actually brings people in.

The fix costs nothing. Ask the question at the moment of sale, in person, on the phone or in the order form. Write the answer down in one place.

After a month, you will have a short list. Double down on the top one and trim the one that never shows up.

This week, add that one question to your first conversation with every new customer.

Where do you think most of your customers come from right now?
