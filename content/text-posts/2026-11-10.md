If your marketing is for everyone, it is really for no one.

Many business owners are afraid to say who they do not serve. So the message stays vague. "Quality service for all your needs." Nobody remembers that.

I see this a lot: the clearer a business is about who it is not for, the easier it is for the right people to say "that is me."

Saying no to the wrong customer is not lost sales. It is a message that finally sounds specific.

This week, write one sentence that starts with "We are not the right fit if…" Keep it for yourself first. Then see how it changes your homepage, your posts and your replies.

Who is the customer you wish you had said no to?
