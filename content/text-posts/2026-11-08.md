Copying your competitor's content is a slow way to look like everyone else.

Many business owners open a rival's page, see what they post and do the same thing, only a week later. It feels safe.

But if a customer cannot tell two businesses apart, they pick the cheaper one or the closer one. You have made price the only difference.

Their content works for their offer, their customers and their story. It was never built for yours.

This week, write down one thing you do that your competitors do not. Then make your next three posts about only that.

Say it plainly. Show it once, clearly, in your own words.

What is the one thing customers say only you do?
