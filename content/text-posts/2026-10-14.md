# Text post — 2026-10-14 19:00 IST

Topic: Posting daily without a goal
Type: honest "what does not work" (general, no invented client story)

Posting every day is not a strategy. It is just a schedule.

I see this a lot. A business owner hears that consistency matters, so they commit to a post every day. A month later they are tired, the posts feel random, and they cannot say what any of it was for.

Consistency is good. Consistency toward nothing is just noise.

Before you decide how often to post, decide what one post is supposed to do. Get a phone call? Start a conversation? Make an existing customer remember you? Each answer leads to a different post.

This week, write a one-line goal above your next three posts. If you cannot write it, that post is not ready yet.

What is the one thing you want your next post to make someone do?
