# Text post, 2026-10-24 19:00 IST: What to ask before hiring any marketer

Before you hire a marketer or an agency, ask them one thing: "What would make you say this isn't working?"

Many business owners ask about price, past work and tools. Few ask how success will be judged. That is where most of the trouble starts later.

A good marketer will give you a clear answer. A number to watch, a time to check it, and what they would change if it moved the wrong way.

A vague answer such as "brand awareness will grow" is a warning. If nothing can be measured, nothing can fail, and nobody is accountable.

Three questions I would ask anyone:

1. What will you measure, and how often will you show me?
2. What do you need from me to make this work?
3. What would make you change the plan?

Write the answers down before you sign anything.
