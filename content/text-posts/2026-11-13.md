Your happiest customers are not telling their friends about you. You never asked them to.

Most business owners wait for referrals to happen on their own. Some do. Most do not, because people forget, or assume you are too busy.

I see this a lot: the people who already trust you are the cheapest marketing you have, and they get the least attention.

Make it easy. After a job goes well, say: "If you know someone who needs this, I would be glad to help them too." One sentence, no pressure.

This week, pick your three best customers and send that message to each.

Who was the last person to send you a customer, and did you thank them?
