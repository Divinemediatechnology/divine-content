# Text post, 2026-10-27 19:00 IST: Fixing the website before running ads

Ads do not fix a weak website. They just send more people to it.

I see this a lot. A business owner is unhappy with sales, so the plan becomes "let's run ads". Traffic goes up, the money goes out, and enquiries barely move. The ad was not the problem.

Think of ads as a bigger crowd walking to your shop door. If the door is stuck, the sign is unclear and nobody knows where to click, a bigger crowd only means more people leaving.

Before spending on traffic, check the basics. Does the page load quickly on a phone? Can a stranger tell in five seconds what you sell and who it is for? Is there one obvious way to contact you?

Fixing those costs less than ads and makes every future rupee of ad spend work harder.

This week, open your website on your phone and ask a friend to find your contact button. Watch how long it takes.

How long would it take a stranger to contact you from your homepage?
