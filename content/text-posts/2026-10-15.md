Topic: Your Google Business Profile is free and ignored
Type: practical tip to do this week (general, no invented client story)

Your Google Business Profile is free, and most businesses never touch it after setting it up.

I see this a lot. A business owner spends money on ads and a website, but the profile that shows up first when someone searches their name still has old hours, no recent photos and unanswered reviews.

When a customer searches for you, that panel is often the first thing they see. It does not need to be clever. It needs to be accurate and alive.

This week, take 20 minutes. Check your opening hours and phone number. Add three real photos of your work or your place. Reply to every review you have not answered yet.

None of that costs anything. It only costs attention.

When did you last look at your own Google profile the way a new customer would?
