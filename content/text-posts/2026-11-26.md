Your customers do not read your "About us" page to learn your history. They read it to decide if you are safe to hire.

Most About pages start with the year the company was founded and end with a list of values.

A stranger on that page is asking one thing: can this person solve my problem, and will they treat me fairly?

Open your About page this week. Replace the first paragraph with who you help, what problem you fix, and how you work with people.

Keep the history to two lines at the bottom.

What is the first line of your About page right now?
