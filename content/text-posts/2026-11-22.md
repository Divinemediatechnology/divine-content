Your thank-you page is the most ignored page on your website.

Someone just filled in your form. They are interested, and they are looking at a page that says "Thanks, we'll be in touch."

That is a wasted moment.

I see this a lot. The hard work of getting the enquiry is done, then the page does nothing with the attention.

Use it. Say what happens next and when they will hear from you. Add one useful thing: a short guide, a booking link, or a question that helps you prepare for the call.

It takes an hour to change and it makes a new lead feel looked after from the first minute.

What does your thank-you page say right now?
