Choosing a marketing agency on price alone is the most expensive way to save money.

The cheapest quote usually leaves something out. Fewer hours, a template instead of a plan, no one checking the results.

You still pay for it, just later, in leads that never come and months you cannot get back.

Price is not the problem. Unclear scope is. Two quotes only compare if both say exactly what you get, who does the work, and how success is measured.

Before you sign, ask each agency to write down the goal, the deliverables and the one number they will report on every month. Then compare.
