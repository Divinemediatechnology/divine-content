Topic: Choosing an agency on price alone
Type: mistake business owners make (general, no invented client story)

The cheapest agency quote is rarely the cheapest option.

I see this a lot. A business owner gets three quotes, picks the lowest, and feels smart. Then the work needs three rounds of fixes, the replies get slow, and the owner ends up doing half the job themselves.

Price is the easiest number to compare. That is exactly why it gets too much weight.

The quote does not show how well they understand your customer, how they handle feedback, or who actually does the work after you sign.

Before you decide, ask each agency the same three things: what will you need from me each week, how do you measure whether this is working, and who will I speak to day to day.

The answers tell you more than the price does.

What is the one thing you would want to know about an agency before you looked at its quote?
