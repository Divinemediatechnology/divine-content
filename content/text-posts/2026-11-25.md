Your marketing does not need more ideas. It needs fewer things done properly.

I see this a lot. A business owner runs ads, posts on four platforms, starts a newsletter and updates the website, all in the same month.

Nothing gets enough attention to work, so nothing gets a fair result.

Pick the one channel where your customers already look for you. Do only that for the next 30 days.

Add a second channel only when the first one runs without you chasing it.

Doing less is not laziness. It is how you find out what actually works.

Which channel would you keep if you could only keep one?
