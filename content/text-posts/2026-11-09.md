Your easiest new customer is probably one you already have.

Most business owners spend their marketing energy on strangers. Ads, reels, cold messages. Meanwhile, the people who already paid them hear nothing after the sale.

A happy customer needs very little to buy again or tell a friend. They already trust you. A stranger needs to be convinced from zero.

I see this a lot: the follow-up stops the day the invoice is paid.

This week, pick your five best past customers. Send each one a short, personal message. Ask how things are going and if there is anything they still need.

No discount code. No template blast. Just a real message.

When did you last speak to a customer after the sale was done?
