Most leads do not say no. They just go quiet, and nobody follows up.

A business owner gets an enquiry, sends a price, and waits. A week later the lead is cold and the owner decides the market is slow.

I see this a lot. The sale was not lost to a competitor. It was lost to silence.

Following up once feels pushy, so people skip it. But a short, friendly message is usually welcome. The person asked you, after all. They are probably just busy.

This week, go through your last ten enquiries that never replied. Send each one a two-line message: a thank you, and one easy question they can answer in a word.

When did you last follow up on a quiet lead?
