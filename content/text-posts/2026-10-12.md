Topic: Marketing rarely fails. It gets switched off in week three.
Type: negative pattern + practical tip (general, no invented client story)

Most marketing doesn't fail. It gets switched off in week three.

I see this a lot. A business owner starts posting or running ads, checks the numbers after a few days, sees no sales, and decides it doesn't work.

But three weeks is barely enough for people to notice you exist. Most buyers need to see you a few times before they trust you with money.

That doesn't mean you should wait forever. It means you should decide the test up front.

Before you start, write down two things: how long you will run it, and what number tells you it is working. Calls, enquiries, saves, visits. Pick one.

Then don't judge it before that date. If the number is still zero after that, change the message, not the budget.
