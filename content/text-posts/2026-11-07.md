Marketing without one owner inside your business usually goes quiet.

Most business owners hire an agency or a freelancer and assume the job is handed off. Nobody on their side is responsible for answering questions, approving work or checking results.

I see this a lot. Approvals sit for a week. Photos never arrive. The plan slowly dies, and the agency gets the blame.

Good marketing needs one person on your side who owns it. It does not have to be you. It just has to be someone with the time and the authority to say yes or no.

This week, write one name next to "marketing" in your business. Give that person one hour a week for it.

If you cannot name that person today, that is the gap to fix first.

Who owns marketing in your business right now?
