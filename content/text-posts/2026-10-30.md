# Text post, 2026-10-30 19:00 IST: If you cannot explain your offer in one sentence, they will not either

If a stranger cannot repeat what you sell in one sentence, you do not have a marketing problem yet. You have a clarity problem.

I see this a lot. A business owner knows the work inside out, so the website and the posts try to say everything at once. Services, values, history, process. The visitor skims, feels slightly lost, and leaves.

Good marketing is mostly subtraction. One sentence: who you help, with what, and what changes for them.

Say it to someone outside your industry. If they say "so you do X for Y?", you are close. If they ask "wait, what exactly?", rewrite it.

Once that sentence works, the website, the ads and the posts get easier, because they all repeat the same thing.

This week, write your one sentence and put it at the top of your homepage and your bio.

What does your one sentence say today?
