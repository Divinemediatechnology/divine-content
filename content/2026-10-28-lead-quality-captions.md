# Post: Quality leads beat quantity.

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

A big lead list is not the same as a good one.

I see this a lot. A business owner asks for more leads, gets a spreadsheet full of names, and sales still do not move. The list was long. The right people were not on it.

Say you have 200 leads who never needed what you sell, and 5 who did. The 5 will pay you. The 200 will take up your team's week.

The stick figures show it. One holds a bucket of rocks and asks if anyone is buying. The other has a small bowl of gold, and every coin ends up in the jar.

This week, write down what your last five happy customers had in common. Aim your marketing at more people like them, not at more people.

Which would help your business more right now: a longer list or a better one?
