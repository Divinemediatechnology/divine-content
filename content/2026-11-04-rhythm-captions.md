# 2026-11-04 — What if you only posted when you felt like it?

## LinkedIn
You post five times in one week. Likes roll in. It feels great.

Then comes week two, and nothing. Week three, still nothing. A customer looks for you and finds a silent page. She figures you have closed.

I see this a lot with business owners. Posting in bursts feels productive, but people do not follow bursts. They follow a rhythm.

You do not need five posts a week. You need a pace you can keep going, a little, again and again.

This week, decide on one posting rhythm you can honestly keep for the next three months, and put it in your calendar.

What posting rhythm could you keep, even in your busiest week?
