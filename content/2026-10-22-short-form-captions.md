# Post 13 — Fewer fields, more leads

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Every extra field on your contact form costs you leads.

I see this a lot. A business owner wants to know everything up front: name, phone, company, budget, role, city. So the form grows. Then the visitor who was ready to enquire looks at seven boxes, thinks "later", and closes the tab.

Each field is another small reason to quit. You do not need the full picture to start a conversation.

Ask for the least you need to reply. Often that is a name and an email. You can learn the rest on the first call.

This week, open your own contact form on your phone and count the fields. Then cut every one you could ask for later.

How many fields does your form have right now?
