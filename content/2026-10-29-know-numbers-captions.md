# Post: Know your numbers.

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

You can run a business without looking at your numbers. You just will not see the potholes coming.

I see this a lot. Money goes into ads, posts and a website, and the only check is a feeling that things are "going okay". Then a slow month arrives and nobody can say where it started.

The numbers are not there to make you feel good or bad. They are a dashboard. They tell you to slow down before the hole, not after you land in it.

The stick figures show it. One walks blindfolded and drops straight into the pothole. The other reads the dashboard, sees the warning and hops over it.

This week, pick three numbers that connect to money: enquiries, how many become customers, and what each customer costs you to win. Look at them every Friday, for ten minutes.

Which number do you wish you had been watching sooner?
