# Topic bank

Use these in order and skip anything already in `posts-log.md`. Each idea is a punchline plus a
suggested visual metaphor. The metaphor can be improved, but keep the scenes simple enough to
animate with stick figures.

1. **Followers aren't customers.** One figure has a huge crowd behind them and nobody buys. Another
   has 5 people and all 5 hand over coins.
2. **Boosting a post isn't a strategy.** One figure fires arrows blindfolded in every direction.
   Another aims at one target and hits the bullseye.
3. **Your website is your shop door.** A door that's locked or stuck makes visitors turn away. An
   open, lit door lets people walk in.
4. **Talk to one person, not everyone.** A megaphone at a crowd gets everyone looking at their
   phones. A figure speaking to one person gets a nod and a handshake.
5. **Reply fast or lose the lead.** A customer knocks and waits while the clock spins, then leaves.
   On the other side the door opens instantly and they're welcomed in.
6. **Hooks matter.** Two fishing lines: the bare hook catches nothing, the baited hook gets a bite
   within the first second (a "3-second" timer).
7. **Don't chase every platform.** One figure juggles 8 balls and drops all of them. Another juggles
   2 smoothly.
8. **Brand is a promise kept.** A figure promises a cake and delivers a crumb, and the customer
   leaves. Another delivers exactly the cake, and the customer comes back with friends.
9. **Reviews are word of mouth at scale.** One whisper reaches one person. Many 5-star signs make a
   queue form.
10. **Retargeting.** A visitor walks past the shop. A friendly wave from the shop window ("Forgot
    something?") brings them back.
11. **Too many choices kills conversion.** A menu with 40 buttons leaves the figure frozen. A menu
    with 1 clear button gets a click.
12. **Long forms scare leads.** A staircase of form fields gets the figure to give up halfway. A
    single step gets them through.
13. **SEO is planting, ads are renting.** Ads light a lamp that goes dark when the coins stop. SEO
    plants a tree that keeps growing.
14. **Show results, not features.** One figure lists drill specs and gets a bored audience. Another
    shows a picture hung on the wall and gets "I want that".
15. **Testimonials over self-praise.** A figure shouting "I'm great!" gets ignored. Three other
    figures say "They're great" and the crowd turns.
16. **Email list = owned audience.** A rented social stage where the floor can vanish, vs a house
    (email list) where the lights stay on.
17. **Quality over quantity in leads.** A bucket full of rocks next to a small bowl of gold coins.
18. **Know your numbers.** A figure drives blindfolded with a dashboard covered. Another reads the
    dashboard and steers around a pothole.
19. **Video beats text in the feed.** A static sign gets walked past. A moving flipbook makes people
    stop.
20. **Consistency in design.** Mismatched signs mean nobody recognises the shop. The same colour
    every time means instant recognition.
21. **Fix the funnel before adding traffic.** Pouring water into a leaky bucket vs patching the
    holes first.
22. **Don't sell on the first date.** A figure proposes with a ring on hello and gets a slap. Another
    gives coffee, then a chat, then the ring and gets a yes.
23. **Clarity beats clever.** A riddle sign gets confused faces. A plain "Pizza →" sign gets a queue.
24. **One CTA per post.** Five arrows pointing in different directions leave the figure spinning.
    One arrow gets the figure walking.
25. **Your best customers are your best marketers.** One happy customer brings 2 friends, who each
    bring 2 more (a branching tree).
26. **Speed of website.** A loading spinner while the visitor's beard grows, vs an instant load.
27. **Local SEO.** A shop hidden in a maze vs a shop with a pin on the map and footsteps leading to
    it.
28. **Engagement is a two-way street.** A figure talks to a wall. Another plays catch with the
    crowd.
29. **Trends fade, brand stays.** A sandcastle washed away by a wave vs a stone house still
    standing.
30. **Start before you're ready.** A figure waits for perfect weather on the shore while another
    swims and gets better.
