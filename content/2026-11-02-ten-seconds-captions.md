# 2026-11-02 — What if your website took ten seconds to load?

## LinkedIn
A customer sees your ad and taps your link.

Then she waits. One second, a blank screen. Five seconds, a spinner. Ten seconds, she taps back and buys from the next shop.

Business owners often spend on ads and posts and forget the page those ads land on. I see this a lot. People do not wait for good websites. They go to the one that opens.

Your website is your front door. If it sticks, the customer never sees what is inside.

This week, open your own website on your phone, on mobile data, and count how long it takes. If you have to wait, fix that before you spend another rupee on ads.

How long does your website take to open on your phone right now?
