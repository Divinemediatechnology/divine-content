# Post 7 — Win the first 3 seconds

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Most posts don't fail because the idea is bad. They fail in the first three seconds.

I see this a lot. A business owner writes something useful, but the first line is a greeting or a company announcement. The person scrolling never gets to the useful part.

Think of two fishing lines in the same lake. One has a bare hook. The other has bait. The fish are the same. The first few seconds are what differ.

Your first line is your bait. It should make the right person think "that's about me" or "I didn't know that."

This week, take your last three posts and rewrite only the first line. Start with the problem your customer has, not with your name.

What is the first line of your most recent post, and would you stop scrolling for it?
