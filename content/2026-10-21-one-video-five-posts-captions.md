## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

You do not need a new idea every day. You need one good video and a plan for it.

I see this a lot. A business owner thinks content means making something new from scratch five times a week. So they burn out, or they stop.

Here is a simpler way. Record one idea in about three minutes. Cut it into three short reels. Pull your best line out as a quote card. Spread it across the week and you have five posts from one recording.

This week, record one idea you already explain to customers all the time. Then see how many posts you can pull out of it.

What is the one thing you explain to customers again and again?
