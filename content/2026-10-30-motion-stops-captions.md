# Post: Motion stops the scroll.

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Your feed post is not competing with other businesses. It is competing with a thumb that is already moving.

I see this a lot. A business puts a clean text graphic on a feed, and it gets walked past like a sign nobody reads. The message may be good. Nobody stopped long enough to find out.

Movement is what makes a thumb pause. A person on screen, a hand doing something, a number counting up. Even two seconds of motion gives the idea a chance.

The stick figures show it. Same crowd, same street. One sign stays still and everyone walks on. The other one moves, and people stop to look.

This week, take your best written post and turn it into ten seconds of video. Show the thing, not just the sentence about the thing.

Which of your posts would you most like people to stop for?
