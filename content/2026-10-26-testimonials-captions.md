# Post: Let customers do the bragging.

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Saying "we are the best" on your own website convinces almost nobody.

Every business owner I talk to has a page full of claims. Quality service. Trusted by many. Best in town. The reader has seen those words a hundred times, so they slide right past.

Proof works differently. When someone who bought from you says what it was like, a stranger listens. It is not you praising yourself. It is a person like them, with nothing to gain.

The stick figures show it. One shouts "I'm great" and gets a shrug. Three others say "they're great" and a customer walks over to book.

This week, ask your three happiest customers for two honest lines about working with you. Put them next to your main button, with their first name and what they bought.

Where does your best customer feedback sit right now, on your website or buried in a chat?
