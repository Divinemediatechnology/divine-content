# 2026-10-31 — What if you replied in five minutes?

## LinkedIn
A customer messages two shops at the same time.

One shop replies in five minutes. The other replies the next morning. By then she has already paid someone else.

Most business owners think customers pick the best shop. I see it differently. They pick the one that answers first.

A fast reply tells a customer you are paying attention, long before they ever see your work.

This week, set a simple rule for your team: every new message gets a reply within five minutes during working hours, even if the reply is only "Got it, back to you shortly."

How long does a new enquiry wait for a reply in your business today?
