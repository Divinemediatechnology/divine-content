# 2026-11-06 — Ask one question (Stickman)

## LinkedIn (profile — Shahbaaz Khan)

You probably track your numbers. But do you know your one question?

Most owners guess where their customers come from. So they spend on the loudest channel, or the newest one, or the one a competitor is on.

There is a cheaper way to find out. Ask.

"How did you find us?"

Ask it at the counter. Ask it in the DM. Ask it on the thank-you call. Do it for a week and the answers stop being a guess — they start pointing one direction.

Then you stop spreading your budget thin and put your time where people already are.

You don't need more data. You need one honest question, asked every day.

What is the one question you could start asking your customers this week?
