# Post 3 — Boosting a post isn't a strategy

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Boosting a post is not a marketing strategy.

I see this a lot. A post does okay, so the owner puts some budget behind it and hopes the right people show up.

That's not a plan. That's firing arrows with a blindfold on. Some will land. You won't know why.

Before you spend a rupee, answer three things:
Who exactly should see this?
What should they do next?
How will you know it worked?

Pick one target, aim at it, then boost. The budget is the easy part. The aim is the strategy.

What was the last boosted post you paid for, and what was it meant to achieve?
