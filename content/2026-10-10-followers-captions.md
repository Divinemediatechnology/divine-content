# Post 2 — Followers aren't customers

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

12,000 followers and zero sales. I've seen it more than once.

A big audience feels like proof that marketing is working. It isn't.
Followers watch. Customers buy. They are not the same group.

Five people who trust you enough to pay will do more for your business than a crowd that only scrolls past.

So stop asking "how do we get more followers?"
Start asking "who is ready to buy, and what do they need to hear from us?"

When did you last check how many of your followers have actually bought from you?
