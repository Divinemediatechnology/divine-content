# Post 8 — Two platforms done well

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Being on every platform is not a strategy. It is a way to be average in eight places.

I see this a lot. A business owner hears that they should be on Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, X and more, so they open all of them. A few weeks later, every account is quiet.

Think of a juggler with eight balls. Drops everything. The same juggler with two balls looks calm, and the crowd stays to watch.

Your time and attention are the balls. Two platforms you actually keep up with will do more than eight you visit once a month.

This week, pick the two places where your customers already spend time. Pause the rest, without guilt, until those two run smoothly.

Which two platforms would you keep if you had to drop the others tomorrow?
