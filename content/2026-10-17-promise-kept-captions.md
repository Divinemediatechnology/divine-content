# Post 9 — A brand is a promise kept

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Your brand is not your logo. It is the promise you make, and whether you keep it.

I see this a lot. A business owner puts a big promise in the ad, on the website, in the pitch. Then the real product or service arrives a little smaller than the promise.

Picture a stall with a sign that says "Cake". One customer gets a crumb. They shrug and never come back. Another gets the cake, and next time they bring friends.

Marketing can make a promise. Only your delivery can keep it, and that is what people remember and repeat.

This week, read your own website and ads line by line. For every promise, ask: can we deliver this every single time? Cut or soften the ones where the answer is no.

Which promise on your website would you be nervous to test today?
