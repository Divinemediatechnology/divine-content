# Post: Your email list is the one audience you own.

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Your social following is not an asset you own. It is a stage you rent.

I see this a lot. A business builds a big following on one platform, then the platform changes how it shows posts, and the reach drops overnight. Nothing about the business changed. The stage did.

An email list works differently. When someone gives you their address, you can reach them without asking a platform for permission. No algorithm sits between you and the person who said yes.

The stick figures show it. One stands on a stage that gives way and the crowd drifts off. The other sends a message to a house of their own, and the lights stay on.

This week, add one clear sign-up offer to your website and to your bio. Something useful, free, and specific to your customer.

How many of your followers could you reach tomorrow if your account went quiet?
