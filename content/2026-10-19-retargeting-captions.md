# Post 11 — Retargeting is a friendly wave

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Most people who visit your website will not buy on the first visit. That is normal, not a failure.

I see this a lot. A business owner pays to bring someone to the site, the visitor looks around, leaves, and that is the end of it. All the effort went into the first visit and nothing into what happens after.

Retargeting is just a friendly wave. Someone looked at your shop window, so you remind them you are still here. Not a shout, not a chase. A short, helpful nudge.

This week, check whether your site can show a gentle reminder to people who visited. Start with one simple message that answers the question they probably had when they left.

When someone leaves your website today, what do you do next?
