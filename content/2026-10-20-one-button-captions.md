# Post 12 — One clear button beats forty

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

A page with forty choices usually gets fewer clicks than a page with one.

I see this a lot. A business owner wants to give visitors everything: every service, every offer, every link. Then the visitor freezes, thinks "I'll come back later" and never does.

When people have to choose between too many things, the easiest choice is to do nothing.

Pick the one thing you most want a visitor to do on this page. Book a call, ask for a quote, whatever it is. Make that the biggest, clearest button and move the rest out of the way.

If a stranger landed on your homepage right now, would they know the one next step?
