# Post 5 — Talk to one person, not everyone

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

If your marketing speaks to everyone, nobody feels spoken to.

I see this a lot. A business owner wants to reach as many people as possible, so the message gets wide and vague. "Quality service for all your needs." It could be about any company, so it stops people for no one.

Think about the last good conversation you had with a customer. You knew who they were, what they were stuck on, and you answered that. It was one person, and it worked.

Write your next post as if you are replying to one real customer. Use their words, their problem, their question.

Say you pick one person you served recently. What is the one problem they came to you with, and have you ever written a post about exactly that?
