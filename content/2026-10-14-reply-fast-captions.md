# Post 6 — Reply fast, keep the lead

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

A lead you answer in three minutes and a lead you answer in three days are not the same lead.

I see this a lot. A business owner pays for ads or posts for weeks, and then a message sits unanswered because everyone is busy. By the time someone replies, the person has already talked to someone else.

Say a customer asks "what's the price?" on a Tuesday morning. Whoever answers first is the one they start to trust. The stick figures in the video say it better than I do.

You don't need a big system. Pick one person who owns replies, and set a goal for yourself: every new enquiry gets a human answer within the hour in working hours.
