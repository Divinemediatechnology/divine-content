# Stickman topic bank ("What if…?" videos, from 2026-10-31)

Use these in order and skip anything already in `posts-log.md`. Each one is a "What if…?" premise for a
business owner, the beats to act out, and the closing line. Keep it a story (fiction), told to "you".
**Never invent statistics, clients or results.** Only use a number if it is part of the story (day 10,
5 minutes) or you can cite a reliable source in the post's script.txt header.

1. **What if you replied to every message in 5 minutes?** A customer messages two shops. One replies
   at once, one replies next morning. The fast one gets the order. Close: "Fast replies feel like care."
2. **What if your website took 10 seconds to load?** A customer taps your link, watches a spinner,
   taps back and buys from the next shop. Close: "Your website is your front door. Open it fast."
3. **What if you only posted when you felt like it?** Busy weeks with five posts, then nothing for a
   month. The calendar fills and empties. Close: "Rhythm beats bursts."
4. **What if your customers wrote your ads for you?** Three happy customers leave reviews; a new
   customer reads them and walks in. Close: "Let happy customers speak first."
5. **What if you asked every customer one question?** "How did you find us?" Day by day the answers
   pile up and point to one channel. Close: "Ask, then double down."
6. **What if your shop had no sign?** A great shop with a blank front; people walk past. Add a clear
   sign, people walk in. Close: "If they can't tell what you sell, they won't stop."
7. **What if you spent your whole budget on one ad?** One big billboard, one week, then silence.
   Close: "Small and steady outlasts big and once."
8. **What if your phone number was hard to find?** A customer scrolls the whole website looking for
   it, then gives up. Close: "Make the next step easy to see."
9. **What if you said thank you after every sale?** A short thank-you message; the customer comes
   back and brings a friend. Close: "The sale isn't the end. It's the start."
10. **What if you answered the same question every day?** Ten customers ask the same thing. You make
    one short video that answers it. Close: "Answer once, publicly."
11. **What if you tried to sell to everyone?** You shout to a whole street and nobody turns. You
    speak to one person and they nod. Close: "Speak to one person."
12. **What if you never checked your numbers?** You drive at night with the dashboard covered.
    Close: "You can't steer what you don't see."
13. **What if your best customer moved away?** Half the sales go with them. You realise you only had
    one channel. Close: "Don't build on one pillar."
14. **What if you showed how you work?** A short behind-the-scenes clip; strangers start trusting
    the shop. Close: "People buy from people they know."
15. **What if your offer was confusing?** A customer reads the menu three times and leaves.
    Close: "If it takes a minute to understand, it's too long."
16. **What if you followed up once?** A lead goes quiet. One friendly follow-up, and they say yes.
    Close: "Most yeses come after the first message."
17. **What if you copied your competitor?** You look the same; the customer can't tell you apart and
    picks the cheaper one. Close: "Be different, or be cheaper."
18. **What if your Google profile was empty?** Someone searches "near me"; your listing has no photos,
    no hours. They pick the full one. Close: "Fill in the free stuff first."
