# Post: Sell the result, not the drill.

## LinkedIn (Shahbaaz Khan profile)

Nobody wants a drill. They want a picture hanging straight on the wall.

I see a lot of business owners lead with their feature list. Motor size, number of speeds, years in business, tools used. It is all true, and most of it is forgotten the moment the reader scrolls on.

Customers do not buy the specs. They buy what life looks like after the job is done. Show that, and the specs become proof instead of the pitch.

The stick figures say it better than I do. One lists the drill. The other shows the finished picture, and the customer says "I want that."

This week, take your main service and write one line about the result your customer gets. Then put that line above the features.

What is the result your customers actually pay you for?
