# WHAT IF YOU ASKED EVERY CUSTOMER ONE QUESTION? — Divine Media Technology. Narrator: Kokoro am_michael. One line = one clause block.
# Fiction / illustrative story, no statistics claimed. Beats counted as line indices; blank lines skipped.
You run a shop. Customers come, buy, and leave.
But you never ask them one thing.
[beat 0.8]
What if you asked every customer, how did you find us?

Monday. One says, a friend told me.
Tuesday. Another says, I saw your reel.
Wednesday. Your reel again.
Thursday. The reel.
[beat 0.9]
You didn't guess. You asked.
And the answers all pointed one way.

So you put your time where they already are.
Ask one question. Then double down.
