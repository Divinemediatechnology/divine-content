# WHAT IF YOU ONLY POSTED WHEN YOU FELT LIKE IT? — Divine Media Technology. Narrator: Kokoro am_michael. One line = one clause block.
# Fiction / illustrative story, no statistics claimed.
You post five times in one week.
Likes roll in. It feels great.
[beat 0.8]
What if you only posted when you felt like it?

Week one. Five posts.
Week two. Nothing.
Week three. Still nothing.
A customer looks for you. Your page is silent.
She figures you have closed.
[beat 0.9]
People don't follow bursts.
They follow a rhythm.
A little, again and again.

Rhythm beats bursts.
