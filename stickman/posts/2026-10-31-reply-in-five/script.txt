# WHAT IF YOU REPLIED IN FIVE MINUTES? — Divine Media Technology. Narrator: Kokoro am_michael. One line = one clause block.
# Fiction / illustrative story, no statistics claimed. [Customer] = a phone message, own voice with the phone filter.
A customer wants to buy.
So she messages two shops.
[Customer] Hi, are you open today?
[beat 0.8]
What if you replied in five minutes?

Shop one answers right away. Yes, come in!
Shop two answers the next morning.
By then, she has already paid.
[beat 0.9]
People don't pick the best shop.
They pick the one that answers.

Fast replies feel like care.
