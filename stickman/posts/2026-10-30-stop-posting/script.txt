You run a small business. And you're busy.
So you stop posting. Just for a while.
[beat 0.8]
What if you stopped for thirty days?

Day one. Nothing happens.
Day ten. Your phone gets quiet.
Day twenty. A customer walks right past your shop.
Day thirty. Someone sends a message.
[Customer] Are you still open?
[beat 0.9]
People don't forget you because you're bad.

Show up a little. Every day.
