# LinkedIn caption — founder voice (Shahbaaz Khan)

The post goes out on Shahbaaz's **personal profile**. It must sound like one founder talking to
other business owners. Not like a brand page, not like an ad, not like a template.

## Voice
- First person ("I", "we at Divine Media"). Plain, direct, a little opinionated. Spoken-English rhythm.
- Talk to a business owner who is busy and a bit sceptical. One idea per post.
- Take a clear side. A mild contrarian line works ("Followers are not a business metric").
- Simple words. No jargon pile-ups, no buzzword soup.

## Honesty rules (hard)
- **Never invent** client names, revenue numbers, follower counts, percentages, case studies, or
  "a client told me" stories. If a number is not real, do not write it as real.
- Experience claims must be general and true for any agency founder ("I see this a lot",
  "business owners ask me this"). No made-up specific scenes.
- Illustrative numbers must be framed as examples ("Say you have 10,000 followers…"), and should
  match what the video shows.
- No false urgency, no guarantees, no "10x your sales".

## Structure (about 600–900 characters)
1. **Hook (line 1–2, under ~200 chars).** This is all people see before "…see more". A sharp
   claim, a counter-intuitive statement, or a blunt question. No greetings, no "Excited to share".
2. **Tension.** What most business owners get wrong, in 1–3 short lines.
3. **Insight.** The one idea, with a concrete example or the video's metaphor in one line.
4. **What to do instead.** One practical step they can take this week.
5. **Closing question.** Specific and easy to answer in one line, so people comment.
6. **Hashtags.** 3–5 relevant ones plus `#DivineMediaTechnology` (max 6 total), on the last line.

## Formatting
- Short lines, blank line between thoughts. 1–2 sentences per paragraph. No walls of text.
- No emoji walls. At most one emoji, or none. No bullet-emoji lists.
- No links in the body. No "link in comments", no tagging people or pages.
- Plain text only: no markdown bold/headers, no ALL CAPS shouting.
- The post is a video: do not write "see the video above" every time. Refer to it naturally at
  most occasionally ("The stick figures say it better than I do").

## Words to avoid
Unlock, leverage, game-changer, delve, elevate, in today's fast-paced world, unleash, supercharge,
dive deep, "Here's the thing", "Let that sink in", "Agree?", "Thoughts?", rhetorical em-dash chains.

## Before finishing, check
- Would a real founder say this out loud? Read the hook once as if it were spoken.
- Is every claim true or clearly an example?
- Is there exactly one idea and exactly one closing question?
- Instagram caption is separate: short, emoji OK, save/share CTA, max 5 hashtags.
